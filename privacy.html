Bayturn Protocol Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Bayturn Protocol is an offline game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information the app stores and what it does not collect.

Information we collect

Bayturn Protocol does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account, network connection, advertising identifier, location permission, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or motion data.

Information stored on your device

The app stores gameplay progress, earned stars, unlocked levels, audio preferences, vibration preferences, reduced-flashing preferences, high-contrast preferences, and tutorial acknowledgement locally on your device using the system preferences store. This information is used only to restore your local game experience. We do not receive a copy of it.

Audio

Audio playback is handled on the device. Music and sound-effect settings are stored locally. The app does not record audio or access the microphone.

Third-party services

Bayturn Protocol does not include advertising, analytics, attribution, account, security, or other third-party data-collection SDKs. It does not use App Tracking Transparency and does not access IDFA.

Children

Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children or adults.

Data retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until the app's local data is cleared by the operating system or you uninstall the app. Since we do not receive or store personal data on a server, there is no remote account to delete.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated before those changes take effect.

Contact
