Bayturn Protocol

Precision parking inside a living mechanical garage.

Bayturn Protocol is an offline spatial puzzle game where every vehicle, bay and mechanism is part of the solution. Read the route, change the garage, and park every vehicle with control and intent.

Why play

  • 30 fixed puzzles
    Five chapters of authored parking challenges.
  • Five vehicle classes
    Mini, Hauler, Volt, Echo and Tandem each solve space differently.
  • Mechanical routes
    Pressure plates, gates, power nodes, lifts, conveyors and rotating bays reshape the path.
  • Think before you drive
    The linked garage clock advances with movement, leaving room to observe and plan.

Gameplay

Choose a vehicle, trigger the required mechanism chain, avoid collisions, and settle into the matching bay. Complete the full route for up to three stars. Progress and settings remain on the device.
